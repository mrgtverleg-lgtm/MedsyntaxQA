Framework search builder

Design options: pick a search mnemonic or make your own

ADropdown + create your own BStart with cards CBuilder sidebar

Option A. The Medsyntax PICO page with a framework dropdown. The last entry, Create your own…, opens a builder above the boxes. The smallest change to the current tool.

Option B. Users first choose a framework from cards, get help choosing, or create their own. Then they fill in the coloured boxes.

Option C. The active mnemonic stays editable in a sidebar: click a letter to rename or recolour it, or press + to add one. Opens with an example custom mnemonic built from your librarian query.
